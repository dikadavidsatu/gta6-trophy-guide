    Roadmap to the Platinum

    1. Phase 1 — Story first (Chapters 1–4 + finale)

      Play the campaign in order: the opening mission Gone Fishing, Chapters 1 to 4, the named missions (Breakfast Run, The Cartel Heist, Child's Play, Headliners, VCIA Takedown, Operation Savage Glory) and the final mission for the gold. Keep a manual save before the finale and before any decision the game presents as weighty — the five inferred missables all live in or near story moments. While at VCIA, watch for the unscheduled-departure moment. Bank: roughly 13 trophies, including 1 gold.

    2. Phase 2 — Strangers & activities

      Finish the stranger strands (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone and the rest) to complete 8 strands for the Strangers & Freaks silver. Sample every activity type once for Master of None, then work the skill and breadth lists: Shooting Range golds (42), Top Fun drone races (14 locations), 20 different race wins, Cal's fishing challenge, the arcade game and bowling. Bank: the strand bronzes, 2 silvers and a stack of activity bronzes.

    3. Phase 3 — Collectibles & exploration

      Sweep Leonida region by region: 3 collectible card sets, WAiNK It at 100 locations, 18 meditation spots, 100 vehicles discovered, all 52 areas explored, and one illegal operation robbed per gang (9 gangs). Pair the sweeps so one trip serves several counters, and tick locations off as you go — the last few percent of any sweep is the slowest. Bank: 2 silvers and the exploration bronzes.

    4. Phase 4 — Cleanup & the 100% grind

      Close the counters: 250 stunts, 50 challenges, 5 bank vaults, $250,000 of marked bills laundered, $100,000 spent in shops, all 8 garage properties bought and 10 vehicles registered in the Scooter Bros app, plus the one-off feats (Combat Focus five-in-one, the partner robberies, the stealth jewelry robbery). Finish with A Long Road — 100% completion — and the Platinum pops with it.
