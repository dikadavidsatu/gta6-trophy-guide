    Roadmap to the Platinum

    1. Phase 1 — Story first (Chapters 1–4, then the finale)

      Play the campaign in order: the opening mission Gone Fishing, Chapters 1 to 4, the named missions (Breakfast Run, The Cartel Heist, Child's Play, Headliners, VCIA Takedown, Operation Savage Glory) and the final mission for the gold. Keep a manual save before the finale and before any decision the game frames as weighty — all five inferred missables live in or near story moments. On any VCIA visit, watch for the unscheduled-departure moment. Expected bank: roughly 13 trophies, including 1 gold.

    2. Phase 2 — Strangers & activities

      Finish the stranger strands (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone and the rest) until 8 strands are complete for the Strangers & Freaks silver. Sample every activity type once for Master of None, then work the skill and breadth lists: Shooting Range golds (42 challenges), Top Fun drone races (14 locations), 20 different race wins, Cal's fishing challenge, one full arcade game and a complete game of bowling. Expected bank: the strand bronzes, 2 silvers and a stack of activity bronzes.

    3. Phase 3 — Collectibles & exploration

      Sweep Leonida region by region: 3 collectible card sets, WAiNK It at 100 locations, 18 meditation spots, 100 vehicles discovered, all 52 areas explored, and one illegal operation robbed for each of the 9 gangs. Pair the sweeps so a single trip serves several counters, and tick locations off as you go — the last few percent of any sweep is the slowest part. Expected bank: 2 silvers and the exploration bronzes.

    4. Phase 4 — Cleanup & the 100% grind

      Close the counters: 250 stunts, 50 challenges, 5 bank vaults robbed and escaped, $250,000 of marked bills laundered through contacts, $100,000 spent across shops, all 8 garage properties bought and 10 vehicles registered in the Scooter Bros app — plus the one-off feats (five takedowns in one Combat Focus, the partner robberies, the undetected jewelry robbery). Finish with A Long Road, 100% completion, and the Platinum pops with it.

    Missables — flagged from the official wording

    A flag means the official description's wording suggests a one-time moment, a branching choice or a fail-first condition. Every flag is an inference and is labelled as one on the trophy itself; the safe countermeasure in each case is a manual save before story moments and first attempts. Names are shown in this section even when the spoiler setting is on — a warning you cannot read is no warning at all. Click a card to open its full guide.

    How the three-step guides work

    Every trophy's detail panel now lists three steps in the same order: How to trigger it — the action the trophy asks for; Where & when — the place, moment or story window it belongs to; Watch out for — the mistake, trap or missable condition that costs a retry. Hidden story trophies keep their steps spoiler-light, and the steps stay hidden with the trophy until you earn it or turn spoilers off. The search box matches all three steps, so you can find a trophy from a word in its hints.
